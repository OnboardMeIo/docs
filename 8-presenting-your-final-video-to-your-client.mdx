The Motion section is very simple. It follows the same logic as the Frame tool.

However, it is lighter and simpler, as the video render is uploaded to YouTube as either private or unlisted. You then simply copy the YouTube link and paste it into the designated field.

The client can directly leave comments on the video timeline.

From your side, there is nothing else to manage — everything is designed to make the process as smooth and seamless as possible.
