To create a new project, go to the top-left corner and click on “New Project”.

Once your project is created, you’ll get access to 7 dedicated pages for each new project:

### The Brief Page

Fill in all the essential information from your prospects and clients.\
(Yes,you can also onboard prospects to put them in the best conditions from the start with a complete brief and a proposed schedule.)

### The Resources Page

Allow clients to upload their files and assets:\
SVG, PNG, PDF, JPG, etc.

### The Planning Page

Give your client clear visibility on the project timeline and progress.

### The Script Page

Create and refine the video message.\
Clients can directly leave feedback and comments.

### The Storyboard Page

Present the visual direction and scenes of the video.\
Clients can directly leave feedback and comments.

### The Audio Page

Present the audio elements:

- voice-over actors
- music tracks

Clients can directly choose options and leave feedback on the timeline during review.

### The Video Page

Present the final video version.\
Clients can directly leave feedback on the timeline during the review process.
