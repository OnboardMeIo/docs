To create a new project, go to the top-left corner and click on “New Project”.

Once your project is created, you’ll get access to 7 dedicated pages for each new project :

- The Brief Page
- The Resources Page
- The Planning Page
- The Script Page
- The Storyboard Page 
- The Audio Page 
- The Video Page
