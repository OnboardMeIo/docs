Once your account has been created on [onboard-me.io](http://onboard-me.io), click on your profile in the bottom-left corner \> “Settings”.

You can customize your platform by changing:

- the language
- the theme
- the button colors (for both the menu and your website)
- the text color

You can also add your social links:

- website
- LinkedIn
- YouTube
- Instagram
- Twitter/X
- WhatsApp

Once everything is set up, you can either:

👉 [Start a new project](https://docs.onboard-me.io/2-creating-new-project)\
👉 [Complete your profile (your offer and optional add-ons)](https://docs.onboard-me.io/10-edit-your-profile-on-onboard-me)
