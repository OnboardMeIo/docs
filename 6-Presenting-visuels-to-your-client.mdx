The Storyboard section is very simple. You just need to drag and drop your images (for example, created in Figma and then exported as image files).

<Info>
  To keep the project lightweight, you can compress your images in batches of 20 using [iLoveIMG Image Compressor](https://www.iloveimg.com/compress-image?utm_source=chatgpt.com) before uploading them into each scene.
</Info>

You can also describe the animations in writing to help your client better visualize the result.

Your client can leave feedback directly, and you can also propose new versions for each visual.

Once everything is approved, you can move on to the Audio section.
