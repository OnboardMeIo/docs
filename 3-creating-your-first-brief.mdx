There are 2 elements in the brief that directly impact the script:

1 — Video duration (this will adjust the structure length in the Script page)\
2 — Specific terms (these will be visible in the Script page and taken into account during script writing)

To add a person to the project, go to the top-right corner of the Brief page and click on “Add a contact”.

Enter your client’s email address, select “Client”, then click “Add \+”.\
This will automatically generate an access link for your client, as well as their password.

To make the URL even cleaner, you can copy it and paste it into a URL shortener (like short.url) to create a shorter link. You can then share this link along with the password with your client.

They will be able to access their onboarding at any time and leave feedback throughout all stages of the project.
