Go to the bottom left of your email dashboard \> Profile.

You’ll be able to add your offer, set your pricing, and include optional add-ons if needed.

You can also showcase your 4 best projects to present them to your prospects.\
Go to the top left menu: “My Projects” \> “Projects” \> “Edit” \> “Feature on profile”.

Then go back to your Profile page to see your featured projects displayed.

This page is designed to give your prospects exactly what they need at a glance:

- Your work and achievements (your skills)
- Your offer and pricing
- Your social links at the bottom (website, LinkedIn, Instagram, WhatsApp, etc.)

Finally, you can share your profile directly using the “Share public profile” button located at the top right.
