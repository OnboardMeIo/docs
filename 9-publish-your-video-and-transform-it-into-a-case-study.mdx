Once the project has been finally approved by the client, you can take the video URL and add it directly to the top of the Storyboard page.

This will allow you to create a full case study including: the final video, the structure, and all the visuals that were created.

You can then present this to future prospects by sharing the project directly using the “Share public link” button in the top-right corner.

No sensitive client data is visible through this URL, and no comments are displayed.
