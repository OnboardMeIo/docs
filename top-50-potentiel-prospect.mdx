**📐 The 4 scoring criteria**

Only professions that are incomprehensible to the general public, with real budgets and an unsaturated market for video. No obvious professions (doctor, dentist, architect) were kept.

| **Criterion** | **What the score measures (1 → 5)** |
| :-- | :-- |
| **🧩 Complexity of explanation** | The general public doesn't understand what this profession does without an explanation. Score 1 = everyone can picture it (doctor, dentist). Score 5 = nobody can explain it in 10 seconds. |
| **💰 Communication budget** | Does the sector invest in its marketing? Proxy: average revenue, typical company size, sectoral culture around communication. |
| **🏆 Market opportunity** | Blue Ocean effect: how many competitors already use motion design video in this sector? Score 5 = almost nobody. Score 1 = it's already the norm. |
| **👥 Prospect volume** | How many professionals practice this trade in the US? Score 1 = fewer than 500. Score 5 = more than 50,000. Determines the total addressable market. |
